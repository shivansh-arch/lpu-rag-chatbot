NOTICE: CAMPUS PLACEMENT DRIVE - AMAZON
This is to inform all final-year B.Tech students that Amazon will be conducting a campus placement drive on September 15, 2026. The drive is open to students across all engineering branches. The minimum eligibility criterion to participate is a cumulative CGPA of 7.5 and above, with no active backlogs. Interested candidates must register on the placement portal by September 5, 2026. For further details, contact the Placement Cell.

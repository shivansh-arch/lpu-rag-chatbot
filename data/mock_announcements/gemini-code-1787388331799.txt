CIRCULAR: CAMPUS CLOSURE FOR DIWALI HOLIDAYS
The university will remain closed for the Diwali holidays from November 6, 2026, to November 12, 2026. This closure applies to all academic departments, administrative offices, and the central library. Regular classes and university operations will resume on November 13, 2026. Hostel residents planning to leave for their hometowns must submit their leave applications to the respective wardens by November 3. Wishing everyone a safe and prosperous Diwali.

CIRCULAR: END-SEMESTER EXAM ELIGIBILITY
All students are hereby reminded of the mandatory eligibility criteria for appearing in the upcoming End-Semester Examinations (Nov-Dec 2026). As per university regulations, students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in all subjects. Furthermore, hall tickets will only be issued to students who have no pending fee dues. Students falling short of these criteria will not be permitted to sit for the exams under any circumstances.

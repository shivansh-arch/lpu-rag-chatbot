NOTICE: SUMMER INTERNSHIP DRIVE - TCS RESEARCH
Third-year B.Tech students are informed about an upcoming summer internship drive by TCS Research for the May-July 2027 cycle. The selection process involves an online coding assessment followed by a technical interview. The eligibility criteria are a minimum CGPA of 7.0 and excellent proficiency in Python or Java. Interested students must fill out the Google Form circulated on the batch mailing list by September 20, 2026.
